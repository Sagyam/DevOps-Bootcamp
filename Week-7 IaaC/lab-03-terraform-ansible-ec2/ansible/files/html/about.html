About this shop

This page exists to prove routing works: nginx matched /about and served about.html via try_files.

The infrastructure recipe: 1 VPC, 1 public subnet, 1 internet gateway, 1 route table, 1 security group with 4 ingress rules (including UDP 443 for QUIC), 1 EC2 instance, 1 elastic IP. Configured by ~20 idempotent Ansible tasks.
